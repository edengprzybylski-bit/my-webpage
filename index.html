My Awesome Website

Name: Eden Przybylski

Date: December 5, 2025

Course: Into To Computer Science

YouTube is a widely used video-sharing platform where users can upload, watch, and interact with videos. It functions both as a business and a social platform, offering content ranging from educational videos and tutorials to music, entertainment, and live streams. YouTube allows creators to build channels, gain subscribers, and even monetize their content. The website also uses algorithms to recommend videos based on users’ interests and viewing history, making it easy to discover new content.

What I like most about YouTube is that I can use it to play music and videos while I do other things. Having it on in the background helps me stay motivated and makes tasks more enjoyable, whether I’m studying, cleaning, or just relaxing. I also appreciate the variety of content available; there’s always something that fits my mood or keeps me engaged. The background sounds and music create a pleasant atmosphere, making YouTube a practical and enjoyable website for both entertainment and productivity.
